Data Protection & Regulatory Compliance

Campus Safety & Privacy Policy

Campus Carpool is built around strict data minimization, role-based privacy, and transparent fare-sharing standards compliant with the India Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 and GDPR guidelines.

1. Sensitive Data Classification

To ensure complete safety for students and drivers travelling to and from VIT Vellore, we classify all platform information into three distinct data governance tiers:

Tier 1: Confidential

User authentication credentials, multi-factor security seeds, and cryptographic signing keys. Protected using salted, one-way cryptographic hashing and isolated server environments.

Tier 2: Protected PII

Student contact numbers, full names, and travel itineraries. Masked from public discovery and shared strictly with mutually confirmed trip members.

Tier 3: Operational

Pickup location, general destination, total vacant seats, and fare split estimates. Published openly to enable transparent student ridesharing.

2. Threat Agent Protection & Security Safeguards

Threat Profile 1

Unauthorized Drivers & Identity Scraping

Unverified third parties attempting to harvest student phone directories or offer unauthorized transit outside university safety standards.

Implemented Security Safeguards:
  • Administrative verification mandatory before any driver account can claim or service rides.
  • Dynamic identity masking that keeps student phone numbers confidential until ride mutual acceptance.
  • Two-Factor Authentication (TOTP) enforcement on administrative and driver access points.
Threat Profile 2

Automated Botnet Abuse & Seat Scalping

Automated scripts attempting rapid-fire bookings during peak exam departures to monopolize vehicle capacity or create artificial shortages.

Implemented Security Safeguards:
  • Atomic, transaction-isolated concurrency controls that prevent double-booking under concurrent traffic.
  • Private room passkeys and invite links to prevent unauthorized catalog crawling.
  • Time-gated cancellation rules to deter seat hoarding and speculative cancellations.

3. Regulatory Compliance Framework

India Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 — Data Minimization & Security:

Under Sections 6 and 8, personal data processing is limited to verified transit coordination. Contact details are never publicly indexed, and reasonable technical safeguards are enforced to prevent unauthorized data exposure.

GDPR & ISO/IEC 27001 Alignment — System Resilience:

In accordance with international privacy principles, our infrastructure utilizes role-based access control, cryptographic verification, and load-balanced high availability to ensure continuous data confidentiality and platform integrity.

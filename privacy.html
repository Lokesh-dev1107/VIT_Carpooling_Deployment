Data Protection & Regulatory Compliance

Campus Safety & Privacy Policy

Campus Carpool is built around strict data minimization, role-based privacy, and transparent fare-sharing standards compliant with the India Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 and GDPR guidelines.

1. Sensitive Data Classification

To ensure complete safety for students and drivers travelling to and from VIT Vellore, we classify all application data into three distinct security tiers:

Tier 1: Confidential

User passwords, TOTP MFA secrets, and JWT private keys. Stored strictly as salted cryptographic hashes (bcrypt) and never in plain text.

Tier 2: Protected PII

Phone numbers, passenger names, and exact departure times. Masked to public viewers and only revealed upon mutual ride acceptance.

Tier 3: Operational

Pickup location (e.g., Campus Main Gate), destination, total seats, and fare per seat. Made public to enable seamless carpool formation.

2. Threat Agent Protection & Controls

Threat Agent 1

Fake Drivers & Phone Number Harvesters

Unverified parties attempting to join or browse carpool listings to harvest student contact numbers or pose as campus cab operators.

Implemented Technical Controls:
  • Mandatory campus driver verification by transport admins before ride claiming.
  • Dynamic phone number masking: passenger phone numbers remain "Hidden until accepted".
  • Two-Factor Authentication (TOTP) on all driver and admin accounts.
Threat Agent 2

Malicious Seat Scalping & Denial of Service

Automated bots spamming fake reservations during exam exodus to deny legitimate VIT students seats to Katpadi Station or airports.

Implemented Technical Controls:
  • Atomic database-level concurrency locks (MongoDB $expr size checks).
  • Cryptographic 6-character private group codes preventing open scraping.
  • Strict departure cutoffs (2-hour exit cutoff and 10-minute host lock).

3. Regulatory Compliance Directives

India DPDP Act 2023 & GDPR Article 5(1)(c) — Data Minimization:

Only the minimal necessary information (contact number and name) is collected for trip coordination. No background location telemetry or persistent GPS tracking is stored.

GDPR Article 32 — Security of Processing & Resilience:

Requires technical and organizational measures to ensure system availability, confidentiality, and resilience against load spikes and unauthorized access.
